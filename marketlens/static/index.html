    RESEARCH CONTRACT
    What this workbench measures
    TWAP
    Equal cumulative target by minute. Unfilled scheduled quantity can carry forward, but each fill is capped by observed minute volume × capacity cap.
    VWAP
    Cumulative targets follow mean volume by UTC minute from observations strictly before the profile cut-off. This is a historical-profile VWAP schedule, not hindsight execution at the realised market VWAP.
    Participation (POV)
    Each minute requests target participation × preceding-minute volume. No same-minute volume is used by the policy. Capacity is imposed separately by the fill simulator.
    Pricing & costs
    Fill reference is the latest non-stale quote midpoint at the minute start. Without a quote, minute market VWAP is a retrospective fill-price proxy with assumed spread. Execution adds half-spread, square-root temporary participation impact and seeded fill noise. The fixed tape does not react to our order.
    Implementation shortfall
    side × Σ filled quantity × (fill price − arrival price) + fees + side × unfilled quantity × (terminal price − arrival price). Divide by initial order notional and multiply by 10,000 for bps. side = +1 buy / −1 sell.
    Risk & interpretation
    Noise-path intervals describe execution-cost sensitivity conditional on one market path. They are not market VaR, causal market impact or expected trading profits. Whole-window paired bootstrap comparisons are available through /api/batches and the evaluation script.
    Known boundaries
    Minute resolution. No order-book queues, latency model, permanent impact, live execution or brokerage connection. Synthetic data demonstrates software behaviour; real historical data must be supplied or downloaded with the included importer. Run locally; this single-user research app has no authentication.
